To Cameron,

I acknowledge receipt of your invoice dated 8th December 2025 totalling $16,940 with the description "Presales 2024 - Work requested by SOPE Clients and SOPE Sales. Availability, timing and deliverables mutually agreed."

This invoice will not be paid. Our reasons are as follows:

1. There was no agreement, quote, or purchase order in place for this work.
2. Sope has no knowledge of these presales activities or any record of this work being requested or approved.
3. Submitting an invoice for work allegedly undertaken throughout 2024 — now in December 2025 — is unreasonable and does not align with the invoicing terms in our contract, which requires invoices to be submitted on the first business day of each month for work undertaken during the prior month.
4. At no point during 2024 or 2025 was this work raised, discussed, or invoiced until now.

If this work was genuinely undertaken and mutually agreed as you claim, it should have been invoiced at the time in accordance with our contract terms. The timing of this invoice, following your earlier correspondence and disputed invoices, suggests it is not a legitimate claim for work performed.

We reject this invoice in its entirety.

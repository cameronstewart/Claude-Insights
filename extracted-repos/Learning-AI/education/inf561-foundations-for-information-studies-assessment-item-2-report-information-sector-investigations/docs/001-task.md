Task
x
You will prepare a report to present to a Board of Management of an organisation from within the information sector. 

The report is to inform the Board of Management about the sector and a role you have selected. The board is comprised of representatives not from the information sector e.g. local government, university board, corporate board, school council etc. 

Investigate and report on an information sector from the list below. 
Choose ONE information sector. For example:
Libraries i.e. public, national or state, special (e.g. law, health, parliamentary, museum, gallery, genealogy etc.), academic, school etc.
Corporate information centres in business, not-for-profit or government settings, knowledge management units etc.
Records and Archives e.g. national, state, council corporate, school, university, organisational etc.
See Module 3 for more suggestions of organisation types within the sector. If you would like to choose a sector not included in the subject modules please contact your Subject Coordinator to negotiate this.

2. In the report to the Board of Management, introduce your chosen sector, including as applicable:

an overview of development of the sector. Consider the historical, social, cultural, political contexts as applicable
a statement of the purpose and activities of the sector 
a description of stakeholders and clients of organisations within the sector
a description of the type of information provided by organisations within the sector e.g. local history records, government or Corporate information, fiction, non fiction, research collection, multimedia resources etc.
a description of the information formats held in a typical collection within the sector e.g. ebooks, databases, ejournals, hard copy resources, film, pictures, maps, artefacts etc.
3. Choose an information professional role from within your chosen sector

 Choose ONE role, for example:
Special collections librarian e.g. rare books, music, maps, language, local history, etc.
Client services or liaison librarian
Archivist e.g. community, university, national, state, school 
Research support officer
Data or information officer
Repository manager
UX (user experience) specialist etc.
See Module 4 or browse employment advertisements for information about more role titles within your sector

              Provide a detailed description of the role including, as applicable:
a description of the role you have chosen from within the sector
a description of the tasks performed by information professionals in the role
a description of the following required for the role:
skills
knowledge
capabilities 
etc. Add further sub-topics as you consider appropriate 
NOTE: the report should not be about a specific organisation or your position, it should be about the sector and role that you have chosen to investigate and report on.

To write this assessment, revisit the LibGuide Research Skills tutorial for report writing, finding resources and referencing.

Follow the School of Information and Communication Studies presentation guidelines: https://thinkspace.csu.edu.au/sicspresentationguidelines/ 



To inform your report, locate and reference at least 8 distinct resources. Do not cite the modules. However,  you may use sources referenced within the modules such as ALIA’s Foundation Knowledge, Skills and Attributes relevant to Information Professionals working in Archives, Libraries and Records Management (2020), and/or ALIA  Skills, Knowledge and Ethics Framework for the Library and Information Services Workforce (2023).

PLUS use current literature (reports/articles/books), web pages or job advertisements as appropriate.



Report structure

Title Page: Not included in the word count, on its own page
Table of contents: Not included in the word count, on its own page
Introduction: Write a brief introduction to the report describing its purpose.
Body of report: 
Description of an information sector
Description of an information professional role within the sector
Create and use your own headings and subheadings guided by the information required for the report. 
Conclusion: Your conclusion should briefly sum up the report
References: The reference list should include references to all resources cited in your report (minimum of 8) using APA 7. The reference list is not included in the word count and should be on its own page.

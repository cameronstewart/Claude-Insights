Infosys Consulting is the worldwide management and IT consultancy unit of the Infosys Group (NYSE: INFY), a global advisor to leading companies for strategy, process engineering, and technology-enabled transformation programs.

We partner with clients to design and implement customized solutions to address their complex business challenges, and to help them in a post-modern ERP world. By combining innovative and human-centric approaches with the latest technological advances, we enable organizations to reimagine their future and create sustainable and lasting business value.

A pioneer in breaking down the barriers between strategy and execution, Infosys Consulting delivers superior business value to its clients by advising them on strategy and process optimisation as well as IT-enabled transformation. To find out how we go beyond the expected to deliver the exceptional, visit us at www.infosysconsultinginsights.com Infosys Consulting - a real consultancy for real consultants.

Requirements

We have an opportunity available for an Information Architect to join our Team.

Location : Perth

Opportunity : Fixed Term Contract , Day Rate, Permanent.

High-level Responsibilities:

Developing a coherent and consistent viewpoint across a large number of information silos addresses enterprise-wide coordination challenges;
Clarify what impact information has on client's critical value streams underpinned by our business capabilities model and set enterprise Information Architecture requirements and prioritisation;
Map and lead enterprise-wide application communication/integration modelling including mapping integration data flows across the entire application landscape identifying opportunities for consolidation and optimisation;
Facilitate strategic decision-making for new and emerging technologies to pursue opportunities, align to business capabilities and value streams and ultimately drive appropriate value/cost and PoC to prod;
Inform technology-agnostic solutions for far-reaching (5-year) business strategies - e.g. document and record management system(s);
Enterprise thinking and influence information solution designs that are scalable and repeatable. Examples:
Define and maintain a consistently available high-level master information objects taxonomy and provide clarity to EAs, SA's, and the broader architecture community and platforms teams;

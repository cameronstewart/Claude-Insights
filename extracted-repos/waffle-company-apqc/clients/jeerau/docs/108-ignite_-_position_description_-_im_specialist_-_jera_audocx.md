JERA AU is looking for is an information management expert to support
them in developing business rules for document management, including
naming conventions, access permissions, minimum metadata requirements
and view/folder structures. JERA AU use a combination of M-Files and
SharePoint, so the business rules would need to specify when these
applications can be used (ie legal documents need to be stored in
M-Files, while internal memos could go in SharePoint). They estimate
that they will require a resource for about 6 months part-time or
approximately 3 months full-time, and the contractor would be
predominately based in JERA AU's office. Another key part of the role is
implementing these business rules, which will involve training for all
employees.

Most important is finding someone who has a good understanding of
document/information management best practice and various applications
(but not necessarily M-Files; training will be provided on this) and can
develop and implement guidelines for document management.

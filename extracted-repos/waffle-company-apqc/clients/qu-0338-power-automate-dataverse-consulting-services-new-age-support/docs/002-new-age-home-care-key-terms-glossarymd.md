# New Age Home Care - Key Terms Glossary

## A

**ACAT (Aged Care Assessment Team)**
The government assessment team that determines the level of care required for Home Care Packages. They assess eligibility for funded aged care services and determine which of the four care levels is appropriate.

**Acquired Brain Injury**
A type of disability that New Age provides support services for, including personalized care in home and community settings under the NDIS.

**After-Hours Service**
New Age's commitment to 24/7 availability where phone calls are answered by real people, ensuring responsiveness to client needs outside normal business hours.

**Aged Care Assessment**
The process conducted by ACAT or My Aged Care to determine eligibility and appropriate level of care for government-funded aged care services.

## C

**Cancer Care**
Specialized in-home care services provided by New Age for clients dealing with cancer, designed to support them in their home environment during treatment and recovery.

**Care Plan**
An individualized plan developed for each client that outlines their specific care needs, goals, and the services to be provided as part of their Home Care Package.

**Choice and Control**
A core principle of New Age's services, particularly emphasized in their NDIS offerings, allowing clients to have input into what services and supports they receive.

**Complex Needs**
Refers to clients requiring intensive support services. New Age can provide 24-hour support for customers with complex care requirements.

## D

**Dementia Care**
Specialized in-home care services for clients with dementia, focusing on maintaining dignity and supporting independent lifestyle choices in familiar home environments.

**Degenerative Disease**
A category of conditions that New Age provides disability support services for under the NDIS, involving progressive deterioration of function.

**Disability Support Services**
Services provided in home and community settings for people with various disabilities, personalized and flexible to help achieve greater independence.

## F

**Family-Owned and Operated**
New Age's organizational structure, emphasizing their personal approach to care provision and commitment to treating clients like family.

## G

**Government Registered Service Provider**
New Age's official status as an approved provider under government schemes including NDIS and My Aged Care, ensuring they meet regulatory standards.

## H

**Home Care Agreement**
The formal agreement signed to begin Home Care Package services, marking the official start date of the care relationship.

**Home Care Packages**
Government-funded aged care services delivered in the home, available in four levels with varying quantities of services and resources. New Age is an approved provider under the My Aged Care scheme.

**High Care Needs (Level 4)**
The highest level of Home Care Package designed to support people with complex care requirements, typically including significant personal care, meal preparation, and medication support needs.

## I

**In-Home Care Services**
Care services delivered in the client's own home to enable independent and comfortable living. Available through government funding or private purchase.

**Individualized Budget**
Part of the Home Care Package system where clients receive a specific budget allocation to purchase approved care services.

**Intellectual Disability**
One of the disability categories that New Age provides support services for under the NDIS program.

**Intermediate Level Care (Level 3)**
Home Care Package level for clients requiring moderate support, typically including personal care, meal preparation, and medication support.

## L

**Low Level Care Needs (Level 2)**
Home Care Package level for people who are still independent in personal care but need additional weekly support for home help or community access.

## M

**My Aged Care**
The government portal and system for accessing aged care services. New Age is an approved provider under this scheme for Home Care Packages.

## N

**NDIS (National Disability Insurance Scheme)**
Federal government scheme providing funding for people with disability to access support services. New Age is a registered provider offering personalized and flexible disability support services.

**NDIS Plan**
The individual funding plan received by NDIS participants that guides what services and supports they can access. New Age works with clients to develop services tailored to their NDIS Plan.

**Neurological Disability**
A category of disability conditions that New Age provides support services for under the NDIS.

**No Waiting List**
New Age's commitment to implementing care quickly when required, distinguishing them from providers who may have waiting periods.

## P

**Palliative Care**
Specialized in-home care services for clients requiring end-of-life care, focusing on comfort, dignity, and quality of life in familiar surroundings.

**Personalized Planning Service**
New Age's service to help NDIS participants navigate available services and supports, ensuring access to appropriate services as circumstances change.

**Physical Disability**
One of the disability categories supported by New Age's NDIS services, with personalized support provided in home and community settings.

**Post-Surgery Care**
Specialized in-home care services for clients recovering from surgical procedures, supporting the healing process in the comfort of their own home.

**Private Services**
All of New Age's care services can be purchased privately without requiring government assessments or subsidies, providing flexibility for clients of any age.

## Q

**Quality In-Home Care**
New Age's philosophy that quality care begins with respect, focusing on maintaining dignity and complete personal wellbeing of clients.

## S

**Sensory Disability**
A category of disability conditions that New Age provides support services for under the NDIS program.

## T

**Time and Materials Basis**
The flexible service delivery model mentioned in consulting arrangements, allowing services to be scheduled in blocks according to client preferences.

**24-Hour Care**
Continuous care services available from New Age for clients with complex needs, provided around the clock in the client's home.

**24/7 Availability**
New Age's commitment to providing services 365 days a year, 24 hours a day, with after-hours support available when needed.

## Acronyms Reference

- **ACAT**: Aged Care Assessment Team
- **NDIS**: National Disability Insurance Scheme
- **NSW**: New South Wales

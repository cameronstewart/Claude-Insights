# JERAAU Document Management: Our Approach 

# What I Heard 

During our recent conversation, I heard several key themes about
JERAAU\'s document management situation:

-   **M-files isn\'t working as intended** - It was implemented about 5
    years ago, but people find it unintuitive and difficult to use

-   **SharePoint has filled the gap** - Many teams use SharePoint
    informally, especially for collaboration with the parent company in
    Japan

-   **Growth has complicated things** - With around 70 people now and
    involvement in additional LNG projects, document needs have evolved

-   **The experts have left** - The people who originally set up the
    system have mostly departed, leaving knowledge gaps

-   **Security is overly complex** - The current permission structure
    makes it hard for people to find what they need

-   **Different departments have different needs** - Commercial, Legal,
    Finance, Business Development, Strategy & Planning, and Community
    all work differently

# What This Means 

Based on what I've heard, these are the key pain points that need
addressing:

**For Users**

-   **Finding documents is frustrating** - People can\'t easily locate
    what they need

-   **Views don\'t reflect how people work** - The system organisation
    doesn\'t match real work patterns

-   **Inconsistent practices** - Without clear guidelines, everyone does
    things differently

-   **Permissions block collaboration** - The current security model is
    too restrictive

# For the Organisation 

-   **Information is scattered** - Documents exist in multiple places
    with no clear rules

-   **Risk of lost information** - Without proper management, important
    documents may be inaccessible

-   **Inefficient time use** - Staff spend too much time searching for
    documents

-   **Adoption challenges** - User resistance due to poor experience

**\
**

**The Approach**

I believe in pragmatic solutions that work for real people. Here\'s how
I'd tackle this:

# 1. Listen First (Weeks 1-4) 

-   Talk to each department to understand their specific needs and
    frustrations

-   Look at how people work, not just how the system is supposed to work

-   Identify what\'s working well (and should be kept) vs. what needs to
    change

-   Map out the current document landscape across M-files and SharePoint

# 2. Create Clear, Simple Rules (Weeks 5-8) 

-   Develop straightforward guidelines that make sense to everyone

-   Define when to use M-files vs. SharePoint in plain language

-   Create simple naming conventions and organisation principles

-   Get feedback and refine until the rules are practical and make sense

# 3. Fix the System (Weeks 9-16) 

-   Redesign views to match how each department works

-   Simplify the security model to enable better collaboration

-   Create role-based views so people see what\'s relevant to them

-   Improve search to make finding documents intuitive

-   Work around department schedules (like finance year-end)

# 4. Help People Succeed (Weeks 17-24) 

-   Create user-friendly guides and quick reference materials

-   Develop simple training sessions focused on everyday tasks

-   Establish an ongoing support process

-   Monitor what\'s working/not working and adjust

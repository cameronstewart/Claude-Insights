## Four Critical Business Challenges We Solve

### 📁 **Information Chaos**
**The Problem:** Your team spends 2+ hours daily hunting for files, often working with outdated versions while the current document sits buried in someone's email or local drive. Compliance audits become nightmares because no one knows which version is authoritative.

**Our Solution:** SharePoint intranets and document management systems with Microsoft Search integration, Teams collaboration, governance frameworks, and automated records management

**Specific Applications:**
- Company intranets with news, announcements, and employee directories
- Knowledge bases and FAQ systems with powerful search
- Policies and procedures libraries with version control
- Document templates and approval workflows
- Project workspaces with controlled access and collaboration tools
- Employee contact directories with skills and expertise tracking

**Example Clients:** SOPE Web Technologies, All Saints Church Newport, Enterprise DNA, InfoXchange

**Result:** ✅ **70% reduction in document search time**

**Technologies:** 📊 SharePoint | 🔍 Microsoft Search | 👥 Teams | 📁 Records Management

**Specific Applications:**
- Expense approval workflows with automated routing
- Leave request and approval automation
- New employee onboarding processes and checklists
- Invoice processing and approval chains
- Contract renewal notifications and tracking
- Customer service ticket routing and escalation
- Equipment booking and resource scheduling

**Example Clients:** TigerBite Australia, Kinatico, Jarr Co, Opal Packaging

**Technologies:** ⚡ Power Automate | 📱 Power Apps | 📋 Microsoft Forms | 🤖 AI Builder

### 📊 **Disconnected Systems**
**The Problem:** Data scattered across different applications creates blind spots in your business performance. Systems that don't talk to each other force manual data transfers, duplicate entry, and version control nightmares. Without integrated reporting and seamless data flow between systems, you're making critical decisions based on incomplete information or spending days manually compiling reports.

**Our Solution:** Power BI custom dashboards and real-time analytics, integrated reporting systems, executive dashboards, and automated data flows between your existing systems using Power Platform connectors and API integrations

**Specific Applications:**
- Financial reporting dashboards with real-time P&L tracking
- Resource planning and utilization reports
- Project profitability and time tracking analytics
- Budget vs. actual reporting with variance analysis
- Executive KPI dashboards and scorecards
- Sales pipeline and customer analytics
- Operational performance and productivity metrics

**Example Clients:** Forge (Formerly Fleetwood Urban), SOPE Web Technologies, GoldenFields Water, Tri-Star Group

**Result:** ✅ **Report generation time cut from days to minutes** | ✅ **Seamless data flow between systems**

**Technologies:** 📊 Power BI | 📈 Excel | 🔗 Power Platform | 📋 Custom Reports | ⚡ Power Automate | 🔌 API Integrations

# Contract Dispute with Ignite/JERAAU: Key Issues Summary

## Core Issue: Contract Misrepresentation and Process Failures

The fundamental issue in this case centers on contract misrepresentation and procedural failures during the engagement process with Ignite (recruitment agency) and JERAAU (client company). The dispute focuses on:

1. **Contract Representation vs. Reality**
   - Initial advertisement presented a 2-6 month engagement
   - Actual offer was significantly reduced (12-16 days)
   - This material change represents a potential misrepresentation

2. **Contractual Process Failures**
   - Ignite expected acceptance before providing draft contract for review
   - Verification system (WorkPro) had technical difficulties that prevented proper onboarding
   - Timeline and start date were repeatedly adjusted without proper documentation

3. **Contract Transparency Issues**
   - Lack of transparency about the actual terms of engagement
   - Confusion about whether a formal offer had been made
   - Documentation requirements changed throughout the process

4. **Legal Status Ambiguity**
   - Unclear whether engagement was as a contractor through your company or direct employment
   - Agency's treatment suggests potential confusion about the arrangement's nature

## Timeline of Contract Issues

1. **Initial Engagement**
   - Agreement with Ignite to source a candidate for JERAAU
   - Signed agreement confirming intention to proceed

2. **Verification Roadblocks**
   - Multiple issues with WorkPro verification system
   - Technical difficulties preventing identity verification
   - Start date rescheduled due to verification problems

3. **Contract Generation Problems**
   - Request for draft contract denied until onboarding tasks completed
   - Onboarding tasks couldn't be completed due to technical issues
   - Circular situation preventing contract review

4. **Final Stage Complications**
   - Last-minute attempts to work around documentation requirements
   - "Welcome to JERAAU" email sent confirming next-day start despite unresolved issues
   - Attempt to begin work without signed contract

## Legal Questions for Circle Green Community Legal

1. What are the legal requirements for contract transparency in recruitment/contractor situations?
2. What recourse exists when a recruitment agency fails to provide contract terms before requiring acceptance?
3. What constitutes proper notice for material changes to advertised contract duration/terms?
4. What are the legal implications of technical verification systems preventing proper contract formation?
5. What documentation should be required before commencing work?

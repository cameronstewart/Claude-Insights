Communication and Project Allocation
Since October, we have made numerous attempts to contact you regarding project assignments and scheduling via email, phone, SMS, and Microsoft Teams. Unfortunately, these attempts received either delayed responses, vague replies, or no response at all.
Additionally, we received multiple customer complaints regarding lack of progress and the time taken to complete deliverables on projects you were assigned to. As a business, we have a responsibility to our clients to address these concerns and ensure projects are delivered to an acceptable standard.
When we were unable to establish reliable communication with you within the required timeframes, we had no choice but to reallocate projects to other team members who were available and responsive. This was a business necessity to meet our client commitments, not a breach of any agreement.
Your Specific Project References
The projects you have referenced were either:
Reallocated due to your unavailability or lack of timely response to our communication attempts
For Achieve, Kimberly Clark, and Derwent, each project was re-assigned due to your unavailability or lack of timely response to our communication attempts (as noted above).
ARPC was not re-assigned. After discussion with you, we agreed for Greg to lead ARPC so that you could focus on Achieve, Kimberly Clark, and Derwent. Assignments were made internally according to capacity and required effort, ensuring all client timelines were met.

In each case, project assignments require mutual agreement on availability, timing and deliverables. We cannot hold projects indefinitely when communication is not maintained.
Verbal Agreement
Regarding the verbal arrangement you reference from January 2025, I do not recall agreeing to an unconditional commitment that contribution to business development automatically guarantees project delivery assignment, particularly when availability and timely communication cannot be confirmed.
Invoices Dated 27 November 2025
I acknowledge receipt of the four invoices you submitted on 27 November 2025, totalling $76,835. Each invoice includes a line item for "Loss of Engagement. Project reassigned by SOPE in breach of 'win the work, do the work' agreement, with sufficient calendar days available to complete delivery."
These invoices will not be paid. Our reasons are as follows:
There is no written agreement or purchase order that The Waffle Co Pty Ltd would be assigned to deliver these projects.
Our contract is for works undertaken and completed, not for claimed loss of opportunity.
The Waffle Co Pty Ltd has been paid in full for all work completed on these projects and for presales efforts.
We reject any claim for payment based on a purported verbal arrangement that was never formalised in writing as required by our contract.
Termination of Contract
Given the breakdown in communication and your stated dissatisfaction with our working arrangement, we believe it is in both parties' best interests to formally conclude our contractor relationship.
Pursuant to the Termination clause of our agreement dated 13 February 2023, Sope Web Technologies hereby provides written notice of termination of the contractor agreement with The Waffle Co Pty Ltd, effective 2 January 2026 (one month from the date of this notice).
Our Position
Sope does not accept your characterisation of these matters as breaches of contract, nor your demands for reinstatement or threatened invoicing for work not performed. We strongly dispute any claim for damages and will respond appropriately to any formal legal action should you choose to pursue that path.
I trust this clarifies our position.

# Kimberly-Clark Project Reassignment: Communication Breakdown Summary

**Date of Issue:** November 2025  
**Project:** Kimberly-Clark  
**Key Personnel:** Trent, Ash, Cameron (Cam)

---

## Executive Summary

The Kimberly-Clark project was reassigned with a stated justification of "client's time constraints and urgency to move forward." However, analysis of the actual timeline reveals this rationale to be demonstrably false. The decision was made while there was still adequate time to deliver, during a period of personal bereavement, and represents part of a year-long pattern of broken commitments.

---

## The False Justification

### Stated Reason
"The Kimberly-Clark project has been reassigned to another team member due to the client's time constraints and the urgency to move forward."

### Actual Timeline
- **11 calendar days available** to complete **12 work days** of project work
- Expert background on this specific project already established
- Timeline accommodated both brief recovery period AND project delivery within client requirements
- Accounting for weekends, the deadline was entirely achievable

### Conclusion
**The stated "time constraints" were fabricated.** The decision to reassign was made for undisclosed reasons, not due to genuine urgency.

---

## Timing and Insensitivity

### When Decision Was Made
- Decision made **prior** to communication
- Decision made while adequate time still existed on the account

### When Communication Was Sent
- Morning of **funeral arrangement preparations**
- After experiencing **3 deaths in short succession**
- While recovering from flu
- During explicitly disclosed difficult personal circumstances

### Content of Communication
Despite acknowledging "difficult time," the email:
- Demanded immediate response "ideally today"
- Pressed for action on unrelated projects (Derwent, Achieve)
- Mixed sympathy with urgent business demands
- Failed to separate informational updates from action requests

---

## Pattern of Broken Commitments

### The Agreement (December 2024)
**In-person commitment made:** "If we help you win the work, we get to do the work"

### Year-Round Pattern (2025)
Repeated instances of:
- Projects cancelled after being won with Cameron's assistance
- Projects reassigned after being agreed and assigned
- Last-minute changes to scope and deliverables
- Breach of verbal and implied commitments

### Business Impact on Cameron
- Declined other clients/projects to accommodate SOPE work
- Lost revenue from cancelled commitments
- Damaged professional relationships with other potential clients
- Financial instability due to unreliable project pipeline

---

## The Core Issues

### 1. **Dishonest Justification**
Using false "time constraints" as cover for an unexplained reassignment decision undermines trust and professional credibility.

### 2. **Disrespect for Personal Circumstances**
Making business demands on the day of funeral preparations, regardless of stated sympathy, demonstrates fundamental lack of professional boundaries and human decency.

### 3. **Systematic Breach of Agreement**
The pattern of behaviour throughout 2025 constitutes a clear breach of the December 2024 commitment: "help win work = get to do work."

### 4. **Mixed Messages**
Stating understanding and empathy while simultaneously demanding immediate action creates cognitive dissonance and reveals insincerity.

### 5. **Lack of Accountability**
No acknowledgment of the pattern of cancellations, no explanation for the actual reasons behind reassignment, no acceptance of responsibility for breach of commitments.

## The "Bullshit" Analysis

**Question:** Were "client's time constraints and urgency" the real reason for reassignment?

**Answer:** No.

**Evidence:**
1. 11 calendar days available for 12 work days = achievable timeline
2. Expert background already established = efficient delivery capability
3. Decision made PRIOR to communication = predetermined, not reactive
4. Weekend buffers and recovery time still accommodated deadline

**Conclusion:** The stated reason is demonstrably false. The actual reasons remain undisclosed, suggesting:
- Preference for another team member
- Internal politics or resource allocation
- Lack of confidence (unjustified by timeline evidence)
- Other unstated business considerations

**Why This Matters:** If the real reasons were legitimate, they should have been stated honestly. Using false justifications destroys professional trust and credibility.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **The timeline math doesn't support the urgency claim** - This wasn't about client constraints; it was about an undisclosed preference or decision.

2. **The timing was inexcusable** - Regardless of business needs, sending demanding emails on the day of funeral preparations crosses fundamental professional boundaries.

3. **The pattern is clear** - This is not an isolated incident but part of a year-long pattern of broken commitments following the December 2024 agreement.

4. **The relationship is damaged** - As Cameron noted: "I am afraid your approach in business damaged our relationship."

5. **Accountability is required** - Without honest acknowledgment of these issues and concrete changes, the professional relationship cannot continue on healthy terms.

---

## Next Steps Required

For the relationship to continue, the following must be addressed:

1. **Honest explanation** of actual reasons for reassignment (not false "urgency" claims)
2. **Acknowledgment** of pattern of broken commitments throughout 2025
3. **Written commitment** to honor agreements going forward, or honest statement that they cannot/will not
4. **Financial accountability** for breach of commitments (invoice for full engagement days)
5. **Clear boundaries** regarding communication during personal crises
6. **Meeting** to discuss these matters in person, as originally proposed

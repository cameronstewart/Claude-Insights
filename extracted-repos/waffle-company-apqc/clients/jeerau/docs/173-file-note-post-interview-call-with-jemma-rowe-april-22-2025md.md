# File Note: Post-Interview Call with Jemma Rowe - April 22, 2025

**Date:** April 22, 2025  
**Time:** 12:43 PM  
**Duration:** 10 minutes 7 seconds  
**Call Type:** Outgoing call from Cameron Stewart to Jemma Rowe

## Context
- Cameron had just completed first interview with JERAAU
- Jemma had texted: "Hi Cameron! Wishing you all the best for today's interview. Would you mind phoning me afterwards?"

## Discussion Points

1. **Role Expectations**
   - Cameron expressed concern that JERAAU wasn't really looking for an employee but more like a partner
   - Indicated this seemed more like a consulting lead than a traditional job role
   - Discussed potential mismatch between job description and actual requirements

2. **Agency Relationship**
   - Jemma explicitly requested that Cameron not cut Ignite out of the deal
   - Cameron acknowledged the request but reiterated this was not his preferred engagement method
   - Cameron reminded Jemma of previous discussions with both her and Meluka about preferred engagement approaches

3. **Other Candidates**
   - Cameron inquired about other candidates in the process
   - Jemma explained the other candidate had a very different background
   - Other candidate had operational Information Management experience versus Cameron's consulting background
   - This suggested JERAAU was still determining what type of expertise they needed

4. **Feedback Discussion**
   - Cameron requested feedback at the end of the process to better position himself in the market
   - Shows Cameron's uncertainty about fit for the role

5. **Additional Work Request**
   - Discussed JERAAU's request for a plan outline to help distinguish between candidates
   - Cameron expressed concern about the amount of unpaid work required for a temporary contract
   - Noted this level of preparation might be expected for a senior permanent role but seemed excessive for a contract position

6. **Decision Point**
   - Jemma indicated it was up to Cameron whether to complete the additional work
   - Left the decision about investing further effort in Cameron's hands

## Follow-up Actions
- Cameron subsequently decided to prepare and submit the requested approach document (submitted April 28, 2025)
- This additional unpaid work would later become a point of contention in contract discussions

## Significance
This early conversation reveals several important themes that would continue throughout the recruitment process:
- Uncertainty about the exact nature and scope of the role
- Concerns about work/compensation balance
- Questions about the appropriate engagement model
- Early signs of scope creep in pre-employment requirements

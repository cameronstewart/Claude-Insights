Requirements:

Assessment tasks should be submitted as a Microsoft Word document. Do not submit a Pagest or .odt document. 
No executive summary or abstract is required for this report.
Quotes should be used sparingly to support the information you are presenting. Limit to no more than 1-2 quotes per submission.
Use language that is appropriate to a professional task i.e. a report to a Board of Management. 
The word count is taken from the first word to the last word and includes tables, quotes and all in-text references.

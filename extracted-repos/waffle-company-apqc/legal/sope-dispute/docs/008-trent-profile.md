Meet the Team 👋 
Introducing Trent Allday – CEO & founder of Sope. Trent has always had a passion for computers and technology, founding Sope back in 2009.
“Since I can remember, I’ve been captivated by technology, gizmos and gadgets. In my teens I was building websites for small businesses, constantly learning new programs, looking to advance my knowledge, and seeking out business solutions that make life better. This hunger to make life easier in the technical space inspired me to start my own business.”
Your Sopies love and admire your hard work, Trent!


Who am I?
Trent is the technical lead at Sope Web Technologies and has installed, supported and restored many SharePoint environments over the years. He lives and breathes technology and is always on the lookout for the latest tech gadgets to solve everyday problems. He built his first website when he was just 16 and went onto Graduate from Swinburne University in a Bachelor Degree of Information Technology & Software Engineering in 2007.  He founded his own website firm in 2003 to later combine with Ian Craig to form Sope Web Technologies in 2009. Sope implement and tailor Microsoft SharePoint to improve business productivity and enhance their clients online identity.

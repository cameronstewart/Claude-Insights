**Most Significant Evidence**:

**Favoring Cameron**:

- Pattern of presales contribution followed by reassignment
- Business impact from declined opportunities

**Favoring SOPE**:
- Written variation requirement in contract
- No written agreements or purchase orders for disputed projects

**Legal Prognosis**: SOPE's position appears significantly stronger due to:
1. Written contract variation requirement
2. Lack of written project agreements
3. Communication breakdown documentation

5. Cameron's documentation and timing deficiencies

**Likely Outcome**: Absent compelling evidence of the verbal agreement and Cameron's responsiveness, SOPE's contractual protections likely defeat Cameron's claims. The presales invoice faces particular challenges due to timing and lack of contemporaneous authorization.

**Settlement Considerations**: Given litigation costs and uncertainty, both parties may benefit from negotiated resolution addressing:
- Partial payment for presales contributions (if documented)
- Structured termination with clear handover obligations
- Mutual releases preventing further claims
- Professional reference arrangements

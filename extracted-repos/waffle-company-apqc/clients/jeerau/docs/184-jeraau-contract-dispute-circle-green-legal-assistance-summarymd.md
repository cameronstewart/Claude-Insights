## Core Issues

### 1. Contract Misrepresentation
- Initial advertisement presented a 2-6 month engagement
- Actual offer significantly reduced to 12-16 days
- Material change represents potential misrepresentation

### 2. Contractual Process Failures
- Ignite expected acceptance before providing draft contract for review
- WorkPro verification system had technical difficulties preventing proper onboarding
- Biometric verification repeatedly failed despite multiple attempts
- Timeline and start date repeatedly adjusted without proper documentation

### 3. Contract Transparency Issues
- Lack of transparency about actual terms of engagement
- Confusion about whether a formal offer had been made
- Documentation requirements changed throughout the process

### 4. Pre-Contract Work Concerns
- Completed approach document for JERAAU (12 hours of work)
- No compensation for pre-engagement work
- JERAAU confirmed they are "unable to make any payments to contractors prior to contract assignment commencement"

### 5. Final Outcome
- JERAAU withdrew their offer
- Cited "lack-of-responsiveness during the pre-engagement process and inability to adequately ensure you were ready-to-commence the assignment"
- No compensation provided for time and work already completed

---

## Legal Questions Submitted

1. What recourse exists when a recruitment agency misrepresents contract duration?
2. What are my rights when asked to work before contract signing?
3. Can I seek compensation for work completed during the pre-contract phase?
4. What documentation should have been provided before I started?
5. Is biometric scanning required to issue contracts?

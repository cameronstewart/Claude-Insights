1.  **PURPOSE:**

> To provide an update on the work completed to date on the service
> directory and the proposed next steps.

2.  **BACKGROUND:**

> "Service Directory" Definition: An internal list of IPC Health
> services or supports.
>
> An Internal Service Directory has been identified as a priority output
> for the SMT project.
>
> Whilst a service directory exists on iHub, staff feedback has
> indicated that this information is not updated in a timely manner, it
> is difficult to search information, and the information is generally
> not trusted (due to not knowing if it is up to date).
>
> An updated Internal Service Directory will be an enabling resource for
> the following SMT action groups:

-   AG3 Referral Forms -- will provide complete and current list of
    services to identify opportunities for online referrals

-   AG4 1300 Number -- will provide valuable resource for call
    responders to know how to answer queries and direct call

-   AG6 Welcoming Clients -- The database of information created could
    also be used to input into information for clients

-   AG8 Holistic needs identification -- will provide complete and
    current list of services to support service navigation

-   AG9 Collaborative Care -- will enable service providers to access a
    complete and current list of services to enable referral and
    multi-disciplinary collaboration.

Concurrent to this work, the Communications team have commenced
discussions around moving the entire Intranet from iHub into SharePoint.
This work directly relates to the Service Directory, as this would be
housed within the new Intranet on SharePoint.

Whilst, completing this work together could be ideal, it would likely
slow down the timeline for the Service Directory, if all other aspects
of the new intranet are being resolved concurrently. However, Service
Delivery project leads will communicate with Intranet working group to
ensure alignment and that any work is considering the bigger picture of
the Intranet.

Note: The need for an updated Staff Directory has also been identified,
however this will be addressed later.

3.  **Key Information:**

The SMT team have been leading the development of a new Internal Service
Directory, considering both where it will live, as well as what
associated procedures and supports are required to keep it up to date.

Actions completed so far:

Meetings with identified stakeholders (November to April):

-   Jo Szczepanska (Digital Health Lead)

-   Ben Letson (ICT)

-   Annette Raijer (Communications)

-   Jane Garnett (People and Culture)

-   Carolyn Uzelac (Manager Communications and Marketing)

-   Joanna Feely (Communications and Engagement Officer)

-   Kylie Thurlow (Manager Governance)

Service information was collected from five teams, including their
feedback on the type of information being included.

Two focus groups were conducted (March) with staff from various back of
house and front of house teams where they:

-   Identified priority information for the service directory

-   Identified preferred platform for the service directory (SharePoint)

-   Identified barriers and solutions to keeping service directory
    information up to date

-   Drafted a procedure for keeping service information up to date

-   Identified technical features desired for the service directory

The following Service Directory elements have been developed:

-   Draft Microsoft Lists (to use as database for information)

-   Draft SharePoint pages (the location of the Service Directory)

-   Draft Procedure -- Service Directory

-   List of technical requirements for the Service Directory

4.  **Next Steps:**

Given the technical expertise and time required to create the service
directory, it is recommended that the development of the Service
Directory is completed by engaging an IT developer to create the service
directory pages and the background automations.
